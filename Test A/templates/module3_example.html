Module 3 Reference & Worked Examples

Note: These worked examples illustrate the core mathematical procedures using distinct sample problems.

Example 1: Linear Equation Isolation

Solve for $x$: $5x - 9 = 26$

Step 1 (Add constant 9 to both sides): $5x = 26 + 9 = 35$
Step 2 (Divide by coefficient 5): $x = \frac{35}{5} = 7$
Answer: $x = 7$

Example 2: Quadratic Equation Factoring

Solve for $x$: $x^2 - 7x + 12 = 0$

Step 1 (Find factors of +12 that sum to -7): $-3$ and $-4$
Step 2 (Factor quadratic): $(x - 3)(x - 4) = 0$
Step 3 (Solve linear roots): $x - 3 = 0 \Rightarrow x = 3$ and $x - 4 = 0 \Rightarrow x = 4$
Answer: $x = 3, 4$

Example 3: Quadratic Formula & Discriminant Analysis

Solve for $x$: $3x^2 + 8x + 4 = 0$

Step 1 (Identify $a, b, c$): $a = 3, b = 8, c = 4$
Step 2 (Calculate Discriminant $D$): $D = b^2 - 4ac = 8^2 - 4(3)(4) = 64 - 48 = 16 > 0$ (2 real distinct rational roots)
Step 3 (Apply Formula): $x = \frac{-8 \pm \sqrt{16}}{2(3)} = \frac{-8 \pm 4}{6} \Rightarrow x = -\frac{4}{6} = -\frac{2}{3}$ or $x = -\frac{12}{6} = -2$
Answer: $x = -\frac{2}{3}, -2$

Example 4: Non-Linear Systems of Equations

Solve the system: $x + y = 9$ and $xy = 20$

Step 1 (Substitute $y$): $y = 9 - x \rightarrow x(9 - x) = 20 \rightarrow 9x - x^2 = 20$
Step 2 (Rearrange into standard quadratic): $x^2 - 9x + 20 = 0 \rightarrow (x - 5)(x - 4) = 0$
Step 3 (Find $(x,y)$ pairs): When $x = 5 \Rightarrow y = 4$; when $x = 4 \Rightarrow y = 5$
Answer: $(x,y) = (5,4)$ or $(4,5)$

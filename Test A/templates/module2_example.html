Module 2 Reference & Worked Examples

💡 Note: These worked examples illustrate the core mathematical procedures using distinct sample problems.

Example 1: Polynomial Derivatives & Power Rule

Find the derivative of $y = 5x^4 + 3x^3 - 8x + 9$

Step 1 (Apply Power Rule): $\frac{d}{dx}(5x^4) = 20x^3$, $\frac{d}{dx}(3x^3) = 9x^2$, $\frac{d}{dx}(-8x) = -8$
Step 2 (Constant Derivative): $\frac{d}{dx}(9) = 0$
Answer: $y' = 20x^3 + 9x^2 - 8$

Example 2: Definite Integration & Fundamental Theorem

Evaluate $\int_1^2 (4x^3 - 6x) \, dx$

Step 1 (Find Antiderivative): $F(x) = \int (4x^3 - 6x) dx = x^4 - 3x^2$
Step 2 (Evaluate Limits $F(2) - F(1)$): $(2^4 - 3(2)^2) - (1^4 - 3(1)^2) = (16 - 12) - (1 - 3) = 4 - (-2) = 6$
Answer: $6$

Example 3: Product Rule & Logarithms

Find the derivative of $y = x^4 \cdot \ln(x)$

Step 1 (Identify $u$ and $v$): $u = x^4 \Rightarrow u' = 4x^3$ and $v = \ln(x) \Rightarrow v' = \frac{1}{x}$
Step 2 (Apply $u'v + uv'$): $4x^3 \ln(x) + x^4 \cdot \frac{1}{x} = 4x^3 \ln(x) + x^3 = x^3(4\ln(x) + 1)$
Answer: $y' = x^3(4\ln(x) + 1)$

Example 4: Integration by Parts

Evaluate $\int x \cos(x) \, dx$

Step 1 (Set $u$ and $dv$): $u = x \Rightarrow du = dx$ and $dv = \cos(x) dx \Rightarrow v = \sin(x)$
Step 2 (Apply $uv - \int v du$): $x \sin(x) - \int \sin(x) dx = x \sin(x) - (-\cos(x)) + C = x \sin(x) + \cos(x) + C$
Answer: $x \sin(x) + \cos(x) + C$

Module 1 Reference & Worked Examples

Note: These worked examples illustrate the core mathematical procedures using distinct sample problems.

Example 1: Order of Operations (Arithmetic Precedence)

Evaluate $180 \div (18 - 6) + 14 \times 5$

Step 1 (Parentheses): $18 - 6 = 12 \rightarrow 180 \div 12 + 14 \times 5$
Step 2 (Division & Multiplication): $180 \div 12 = 15$ and $14 \times 5 = 70 \rightarrow 15 + 70$
Step 3 (Addition): $15 + 70 = 85$
Answer: $85$

Example 2: Common Denominators & Fraction Operations

Evaluate $\frac{2}{5} + \frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{2}$

Step 1 (Find LCD): Least Common Denominator of $5, 4, 2$ is $20$.
Step 2 (Convert fractions): $\frac{2 \times 4}{20} + \frac{3 \times 5}{20} - \frac{1 \times 10}{20} = \frac{8}{20} + \frac{15}{20} - \frac{10}{20}$
Step 3 (Combine numerators): $\frac{8 + 15 - 10}{20} = \frac{13}{20}$
Answer: $\frac{13}{20}$

Example 3: Complex Fraction Division

Simplify $\frac{\frac{5}{12}}{\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{2}{5}}$

Step 1 (Denominator Multiplication): $\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{2}{5} = \frac{6}{20} = \frac{3}{10}$
Step 2 (Invert and Multiply): $\frac{5}{12} \div \frac{3}{10} = \frac{5}{12} \times \frac{10}{3} = \frac{50}{36} = \frac{25}{18}$
Answer: $\frac{25}{18}$

Example 4: Rational Algebraic Equations

Solve for $x$: $\frac{4}{x+2} + \frac{1}{x} = \frac{7}{x+2}$

Step 1 (Group like terms): Subtract $\frac{4}{x+2}$ from both sides $\rightarrow \frac{1}{x} = \frac{7 - 4}{x+2} = \frac{3}{x+2}$
Step 2 (Cross Multiply): $x+2 = 3x \rightarrow 2x = 2 \rightarrow x = 1$
Answer: $x = 1$
